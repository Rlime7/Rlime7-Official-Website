Silver Dust (Iron Rust)

Even though it has a weird name, it's a reference to a gift I got for a friend, and the cover art features said gift (just blurred and distorted, lol). I made it around August 2025, and it's very C418-inspired.

This song almost didn't see the light of day because its project file got corrupted, and I spent half a day trying to recover it. It also takes some inspiration from my own song, "Citywalk Reverie", and I personally really like how it turned out. Also, this and "One Coffee Please" are probably my favourite songs I've made.
