Last Summer

This was basically me realizing that I have no summer songs. I mean, sure, you can listen to any song anytime, but I kind of wanted something more groovy in a way. Stylistically, this is also the catalyst for Autumnfest, as I was really into using chiptune instruments for bass at that time.

Originally it was a lot more repetitive, but a friend pointed that out and I tried my best to add some variation. I hope it worked.

Anyway, the cover art was taken in the same location as the "Serenity Overdrive" one. Except it wasn't even taken in summer, but in the middle of autumn 2024, lol.

Originally I didn't even want to release this song, as I thought I had some better projects at the time. Eventually, this song grew on me a lot and became one of my favourites. Some of those "other songs" went on Autumnfest, and some are still waiting to be released in future albums.
