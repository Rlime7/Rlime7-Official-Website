Hope Dies Last

Back in September–December 2023, the roots of me starting to listen to rock music began forming for various reasons, such as it being a messed up year, getting a crush on a girl who was into this stuff, and some friends recommending me various songs.

I didn't completely understand this genre yet, and I didn't have either the knowledge or the means to make anything in this genre, but... I still tried. An abomination consisting of 100% VSTs and 0% actual guitars or drums that somehow sounds actually decent...? (even though it has nothing to do with rock lol).

In fact, this is my first release that I actually cherish, and I consider it the beginning of my new era. It was also meant to be the first single of an EP, perhaps even an LP, that never saw the light of day, the only other exception being "I Just Wanna Be Free".

A fun fact (not so fun to me) is that at the time I didn't really know anything about mixing and how loud things should be, so I tried doing it by ear on as many speakers as possible.

The problem comes from the fact that at the time I had just bought a speaker that was pretty okay, but not for kicks. Those sound horrible on it, like they come out of a tin can, and I tried so hard to tweak this song in mixing that in the end it ended up worse (I think) than before.

Due to this fact, the kick drum might be a bit too loud — sorry about that..
