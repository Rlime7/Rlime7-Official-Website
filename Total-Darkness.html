Total Darkness

The third and final single for Electronic Dystopia.tmp (Vol. 1) was made 99% on my laptop because the button on my PC's power supply decided to break.

This project started around April 2024, and for a few days (or a week — I don't remember if I finished it before or after going to the mountains for a few days) it was just a bunch of chords and a drum beat. Somehow, that rhythmic chord progression got stuck in my brain, and I began feeling like it needed to be more than that.

Besides the melody, I played that groovy bassline directly on the laptop's keyboard and recorded it, and later I tried adding a counter-melody that ended up becoming a song on its own ("My Melody, My Memory").

Also, because it was made on my laptop and I have almost no downloaded plugins there, it was made almost entirely with stock plugins and samples.
