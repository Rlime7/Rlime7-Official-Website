Citywalk Reverie






Back on the 4th of April 2025 I went to see the Minecraft Movie with some friends, the day it came out. The movie was fine, but the true experience that ignited my inspiration for this song only happened after.

After I parted ways with my friend group, I did something I've somehow never done before that moment. I went on a long ass walk from the moment I left them, through sunset, and until the darkness engulfed the streets, all the way until I reached the exit from my city to a highway. Somehow this experience felt life-changing in a way, like it cleansed my mind and made me a new person. I don't know why I've never done this alone before, but after this I clearly repeated the experience and I loved it every single time.

I also took a lot of pictures that day, the cover art of this song being one of them. The entire time, I listened to a playlist of mine containing songs that feel like solitude and sunset, and this played an important role in giving me inspiration for this song, as I kind of merged the vibe of that entire playlist into one.

The song itself was made like ten days later, but it had in mind that exact day when I decided to part ways from my friends and go all alone on that walk. At first it started as just that incredible drop at the end, and I extended it from there with the other two choruses and the ambient parts. I made it all in like 2 days. More accurately, I started it one night and continued it the next morning, finishing it by noon.

I also remember playing C418's new game *Wanderstop* that morning, and that was also some good inspiration for what would become my absolute favourite song from anything I've ever written.

Also, if I had made it earlier, it would have been on Volume 2 of my album "Electronic Dystopia.tmp", as I think its style would have fit nicely alongside "Serenity Overdrive" and "Going Home".
