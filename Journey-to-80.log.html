Journey to 80.log

Probably my favourite song across this entire project, so much so that when I first made it I thought of it as flawless. It's a synthwave song where I tried to have fun and used some samples here and there (OH SHI-, HERE WE GO AGAIN).

If I am not mistaken, this is the last song I made before leaving for holiday in Turkey, literally finishing it the day before. I just tried having fun with as many arpeggios and rhythmic chords as possible. It seemed to do wonders, and the fact that this is my most popular non-Minecraft song confirms that.

The cover art picture was taken where I went for holiday.

Also, the title is meant to imply both a journey to 1980, the stereotypical core of synthwave, and 2080, which would seem more appropriate with the futuristic aesthetic from our current point of view.
