I Just Wanna Be Free

As I mentioned on the "Hope Dies Last" page, in December 2023 I actually started listening to some light rock, mostly indie stuff and post-punk.

This song tries to be exactly that, even though, once again, it uses only VSTs and no real instruments. I think it's a step up in quality from the last one, probably also because I have around 20 hours spent in this project, as it went through many iterations (thank God I actually went with a Linn kick drum and not the same one as on "Hope Dies Last").

A lot of that time was also spent trying to make some deep-fried vocals recorded with my phone while I was sick sound good.

I still consider this song my most majestic one to this day, melody- and counter-melody-wise, and I really hope you can agree with me on this.
