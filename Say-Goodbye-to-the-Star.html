Say Goodbye to the Star

"Say Goodbye to the Star" has two meanings. The first is that it's a New Year's Eve song, and you're saying goodbye to the last rotation of Earth around our star. The second is kind of about me (I am the star, lmao), as I wanted to take a short break and focus on the second half of my final year of high school. It's a very synthwave-inspired song that feels like a warm goodbye.

It also has a slightly older unreleased sibling, similar in style yet still quite different, which will probably be my next release.

The cover art was photographed in my hometown using my phone and then slightly edited.

For the visualiser, I tried to warp the cover art in a way that mimics the event horizon of a black hole.
