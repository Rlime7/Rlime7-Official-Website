Love Across The Universe

My first released single, Love Across The Universe, "an experimental House Music track that starts with a guitar intro that builds into an energic synth based experience" as I described it on Soundcloud, was made somewhere in February 2023 and then the part that took me the most time was the cover art, lol. It was definetely not the first track I've ever made, not even the first one made in Fl Studio. Before it, I've had like 3-4 albums worth of music that I wanted to release but I think it's better that I did not, as it was not that great. Most of it was made in LMMS, a free open source daw that is kinda decent but Love Across the Universe was my first song that I thought (at least at the time) was worth showing to the world.
