Timeless Echoes

This was the first ever released single for what would become my biggest project yet, a 31-song album split into two volumes. I still can't believe I did this.

This song in particular takes a lot of inspiration from the legend himself, C418, mostly from Timelapse Kingdom, a progressive minimal house song from 2011.

This was a huge leap in quality from my last release, as it came out after a long hiatus with no new released music — a hiatus in which most of this album was born (plus some tracks that would later end up on Autumnfest).
