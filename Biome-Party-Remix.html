For the 10th anniversary of C418's album 148, released in December 2015, we as a community decided to remix the entire thing, each person doing a different track.

This was not the first remix I have ever done, but it's the most successful at not being completely horrible.

I personally chose this track because, unironically, it was one of my least favourites. Don't get me wrong, I still like it a lot. I just made sure that if I ended up ruining the song for myself by trying to remix it, it wouldn't be one that I really loved.

I tried to take parts from both the 148 version and the Minecraft: Volume Beta version, combining them and adding elements of my own.

Oh, plus I almost forgot the "It's C418, bitch!" bit that I took from a very obscure and old song of his from 2008, which is not even available on his Bandcamp anymore.
