Serenity Overdrive

The final single for Electronic Dystopia.tmp (Vol. 2) was slightly older, dating back to March 2024. It began as just a small loop, which later became the first chorus, and I added the ambient parts after.

I tried to make it feel cinematic in a way, with the chorus part near the end, slow build-ups with strings, and so on. It also ended up being the second-longest song in this project.

The cover art was taken at a lake I like to visit sometimes.
