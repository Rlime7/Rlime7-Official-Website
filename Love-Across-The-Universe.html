Love Across The Universe

My first released single, "Love Across The Universe", "an experimental house music track that starts with a guitar intro that builds into an energetic synth-based experience" as I described it on SoundCloud, was made somewhere in February 2023, and then the part that took me the most time was the cover art, lol.

It was definitely not the first track I've ever made, not even the first one made in FL Studio. Before it, I had like 3–4 albums worth of music that I wanted to release, but I think it's better that I did not, as they were not that great.

Most of them were made in LMMS, a free open-source DAW that is kind of decent, but "Love Across The Universe" was my first song that I thought (at least at the time) was worth showing to the world.

I also had some lyrics written for it but I think it's better if they stay hidden. I might make a remake to this song some day but no promises.
