Ok, so this is a weekly series that I started on the fifth of May 2023, while the idea came to my mind somewhere between the first and the third of May (can't remember lol), while I was in bed trying to sleep.

Its purpose is mainly for people to discover my work through the unknown ways of the YouTube algorithm...
I hope it at least worked...

Anyway, some random fun facts:

Track 1 was made during a Teams meeting, and because of the way Windows handles audio drivers, I was basically hearing almost nothing — just a very lo-fi version of what I was doing.

Track 7 is a farewell to my grandma, who died around that time from fighting an unforgiving illness.

Track 8 is kind of a collab (mostly just me liking an uncanny track that a friend made and experimenting with it).

Track 9 could've been part of an album that I was working on since 2022, but the only part of it that reached the surface was the main concept, which became Electronic Dystopia.tmp and a refurbished "(Old) Memories".

Track 11 is basically older than most, yet I decided to publish it kind of late.

Tracks 12 and 13 were made while I was on holiday in Turkey.

Track 14 was meant to be the original ending.

Track 15 could've been the OST to a game whose dev I've never heard from ever again.

Track 16 is basically me saying "Screw this, I'm out".
