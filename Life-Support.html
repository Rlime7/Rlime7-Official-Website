Life Support

The second single for Electronic Dystopia.tmp (Vol. 1) is a phonk track that was born after me and a friend worked on a phonk collab that ended up being abandoned, but I still wanted to make something in that style, so I did.

Even though it was released in December 2024, it was made around the same time as "I Just Wanna Be Free", alongside the song "Is It Over?" from Autumnfest, all the way back in February 2024.

The intro features my actual recorded heartbeat, and so does the cover art, in the form of the audio wave. The name and overall aesthetic idea were suggested by another friend, and I liked it a lot, so I went with it.
