Electronic Dystopia.tmp (Vol.1)

The first half of my biggest project yet, a collection of diverse electronic songs that have a slight heaviness to them, hence the album name. Originally, there was no album, just a bunch of unfinished projects that stacked up in my folders and led nowhere.

Then I started realizing the potential of an album, originally having just 7 songs, then 14, and by the end 31.

The first 5 tracks from the tracklist are part of the original 7. Except "Dance 'till Morning" is actually the oldest on this volume, originally created around the same time as "Everything You Do". It was part of a 4–5 track EP consisting only of house songs, but I kind of abandoned the project. In fact, this track didn't even have a name — I just borrowed it from another track from the EP, kind of as a reference to it. I sure changed it a lot for the album, but the core is still there.

"Insane" wasn't added to the tracklist until much later, after I had a song stuck in my brain. Then I realized it was a 20-second fragment I made myself, so I finished it and threw it in there.

The title tracks are kind of the catalyst of the whole project (yet the ambient-ish interlude version was made later, and originally "Electronic Dystopia.log" had exactly the same name as the album, before I split it across the two songs).

"Scattered Brain" is on there just because a friend liked it very much, so I added it — why not.

Track 10 is inspired by C418's album *Excursions*, just like some other newer songs of mine (I love that album sooo much).

Track 11 is again an old one that I thought would fit in, dating back to around January 2024, I think.

"Memento Mori" is a reminder that we will all be dead someday, just like someone I only knew through my dad but still felt close to, who died pretty young in his sleep on Christmas Eve.
