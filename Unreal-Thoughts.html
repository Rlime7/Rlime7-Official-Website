Unreal Thoughts

Unreal Thoughts, my first EP...

Consisting of four songs (two really, lol), it tries to be a dark and ominous piece of ambient music with some minimal drums here and there, but it kind of fails a bit due to my lack of skill at the time. It was heavily inspired by a genre that I discovered at the beginning of 2023. Here are some examples: Example 1, Example 2.

These two are some of my favourites and I kind of wanted to achieve that... Did I manage to achieve that? I don't know, you tell me. Anyway, my personal favourite is `This isn't real`, as it represents this genre the best.

I started working on this somewhere in late January 2023 and kind of just left the files sitting there until May...
