Autumnfest - Our Decisions Lead Us Here

Autumnfest is pretty much a late birthday gift for a friend of mine, plus some extra stuff from around the time when I was still making Electronic Dystopia.tmp. It's the third album I've released in one year, and it consists of both ambient and energetic quirky tunes that, weirdly enough, feel like a cozy autumn day.

"Our Decisions Lead Us Here" was made around May 2025, I think, late at night while I was home alone and in a voice chat with a guy from a server. At that time, it wasn't for anything specific; I just tried to do something a bit spooky and use my voice in a funky way. I think it works great as an album intro!

Track 3 is the oldest one here, dating back to around December 2023–January 2024. I kept thinking about whether I should release this or "Life Support" first (as singles at that time, of course), but in the end, it was neither.

"One Coffee Please!" is one of my favourites on here and the favourite of the person this album was meant for. Its title is also a reference to the fact that said person REALLY likes their coffee.

"Autumnfest", the title track, is the youngest one here. I made it on September 5th while on a voice call with a friend, multitasking while playing some stupid game about making Turkish kebab. Kind of a real contrast to the actual vibe of the song, huh...

Anyway, track 6 is inspired by the Celeste OST, an indie platformer game with a soundtrack made by Lena Raine. It's also a "thank you" to the person who gave me the image for the cover art, the same person mentioned for track 4.

"Welcome To 2025 (You Are Late)" was made about 3–4 hours into the new year that was 2025 at the time. I just missed the opportunity to release it until around September, hence the "You Are Late" part of the title.

"Impostor Syndrome" is a real thing I sometimes feel. Even with this song, I didn't even remember making it when I heard it for the first time months later. I made it somewhere around October 2024 to help a friend who wanted to learn how to make beats at the time, then I kind of forgot about it, only to find it again around July–August 2025 and be like, "Hol' up, did I make that?"

"Lost Memories" is a sweet song, very dear to my heart, along with its "bonus" synthwave version. I just never touched it again after the state I found it in, too afraid it would ruin its vibe, and that's why it's only about a minute and a half long. It prepares you for goodbye.

"From Green To Yellow" is the actual goodbye (or is it?), originally over 7 minutes long and made around March 2024, Lena Raine-inspired and without really having a place until this moment. The "when can I hear the next one?" bit at the end was, I'll admit, inspired by Billie Eilish, but it's also ironic in a way, considering there's a bonus track after it, plus the single I released in December and the C418 remix. At that time, I really thought this would be the real goodbye for a while.

A small fun fact: I think "Lost Memories", along with its alternative version, should've switched places with "Familiar Coldness", purely from a stylistic point of view. But who cares now? Both albums are done and over.
