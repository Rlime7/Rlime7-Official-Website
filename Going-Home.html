Going Home

"Going Home" was the first released single for Volume 2 of my album, "Electronic Dystopia.tmp", and I began the project on the same day as the song "Electronic Dystopia.log".

The goal I had in mind for this one was that I kind of tried to combine the style of C418's "Excursions" with the style of a small indie singer I listened to a lot during that time. I think I kind of succeeded, but it could've been slightly darker if you ask me now.

Anyway, this song is best listened to while in a car, going places or coming back from them — it's your own choice.

The cover art is literally me taking a picture of the reflection of the sunset on the car's window and paint, because it looked pretty, it fit, and I'm pretty sure I was literally testing this song to see if it sounded good while taking this picture.
