Electronic Dystopia.tmp (Vol.2)

The second half of my biggest project yet, that's even bigger than the first volume (and perhaps even better), exists purely because I was lazy. Lazy to what, you may ask? RENDER 31 VISUALISERS, RAHHH.

I thought the most sensible decision to not overwork myself was to split all the songs across two volumes, which also gave me more time to work on some songs and make some new ones for Vol. 2.

The first track was one of those songs that were not ready. I had most of the groundwork done, but I had no idea how to continue it, so I asked a friend (who was at some point one of my favourite artists, and I was purely a fan — and now we send each other cat reels, lmao) to help me finish that song, and this is how it became a collab.

The second song might seem lame after the intro, but isn't this exactly how life goes?

Track 3 is on here because another friend liked it very much and thought it's sooo cool and epic videogame soundtrack-like.

Track 4's name might be related to "Hope Dies Last", but I don't exactly remember in what way, sorry about that.

"Shadows" is an older song and, surprisingly enough, it was meant to be part of the same EP/album as "Hope Dies Last" and "I Just Wanna Be Free".

"Nobody's Watching, So Why Bother" is exactly that. Why should I bother to end a song when I can let the rain do that, which also fades into the next rainy songs?

"(Old) Memories" was at some point called just "Memories" and it's technically the oldest song from the entire project, dating back to October 2022. Besides the overall concept and aesthetic of the album, it's the only thing that kind of made it out to the public from an old unreleased album of mine called "7 Years In Future". In fact, this album's cover art contains the cover art of the old one as one of the billboards. I'll let you guess which one.

It's a total remake of the original, from LMMS into FL Studio, and I changed some stuff and added some extra elements, such as the melody in the second chorus that also ends the song, and the choir, which is actually my voice pitched up, edited, and used as an instrument.

"Shining Stars" is the first song I made after coming back from my holiday in Turkey, and it has since become one of my favourite songs on this album.

"A Strange Thought" is a reference to C418's remix of the Stranger Things soundtrack, "Stranger Think". You could think of it as a remix of a remix, even though I've made it from the ground up.

Track 13 is pretty much inspired by the Phasmophobia lobby OST from the little jukebox in there. I tried to be as experimental as possible with it, but it ended up kinda boring.

"Find True Love" is a bit of an older one, from around December 2023–January 2024, and it's inspired by the piano cover of "Where Is My Mind" by Pixies, which is a song very dear to my heart for reasons I'd rather keep to myself.

"My Melody, My Memory" as a concept was an idea that came to me while in Turkey, and I tried to begin the project there on my laptop, but I didn't manage to do that much. I finished it later at home on my PC, and it's pretty much a culmination and sum-up of everything up to that point from both volumes.

It uses the scrapped counter melody from "Total Darkness", its chords if I am not mistaken, and the melodies from "Electronic Dystopia.log" and "Going Home" as Easter eggs. Originally it was higher pitched and not so time-synced, but for some reason I didn't like it that way and thought it was boring, so I threw a time manipulation effect on the master, tweaked it a bit, and voila.

You could consider it the end for Vol. 2, while "Familiar Coldness" is the end to the entire project as a whole. It's very similar in style to my EP "Unfinished Dreams" and my music disc "Winter Love", hence the name.

I hope I did good enough on this project as a whole, and I hope you enjoyed it!

A last random fun fact: on my 18th birthday, a close friend gifted me this exact album on CD, along with some money. Holy well thought gift!
